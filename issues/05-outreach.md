# TVNL modernization — formal outreach

## Objective

Reach the TVNL Managing Director and relevant IT/procurement officers with a formal, urgent but evidence-based proposal.

## Work

- Address the formal proposal to TVNL HQ and the Managing Director.
- Lead with the public website as a compliance and stakeholder-access channel: tenders, RTI/public disclosures, CSR, environmental information, notices and reports.
- Use the observed EOL PHP/template stack as a factual modernization trigger, without claiming the current site is broken.
- Show the scrollytelling prototype, capability statement, quotation and phased SOW.
- Identify the IT issuing officer from current NIT 22/IT or NIT 32/IT documents.
- Plan an in-person Ranchi follow-up and a formal referral through approved channels.
- Mention JBVNL’s digital investment as evidence of departmental ambition, not as a demand to match its scale.

## Message boundary

Urgency may describe compliance visibility, funding/stakeholder confidence and the age of the public-facing stack. Do not claim the site fixes JBVNL dues, coal-block risk, JSERC filing deficiencies or operational performance.

## Acceptance criteria

- Formal email and attachments reviewed by an authorized sender.
- Recipient, official channel and attachment list confirmed.
- Outreach log records date, response and next action.
- No external message is sent from this issue without an authorized sender and final review.
