# TVNL modernization — procurement and compliance path

## Objective

Establish a procurement-ready route for the proposed service without promising an exemption, GeM category, hosting arrangement or certification that has not been verified.

## Work

- Confirm TVNL’s Jharkhand procurement policy, internal purchase manual and delegation of powers for services at the proposed value.
- Check whether an applicable GeM website/CMS service category exists and whether TVNL uses GeM.
- Check the Local Purchase Committee route only as TVNL’s authorized procurement team directs.
- Explore JAP-IT empanelment through its formal process.
- Prepare legal seller identity, GST/MSME and capability documentation.
- Confirm public-site Indian hosting, CMS/backend residency, DNS ownership and data processors.
- Build a GIGW-oriented conformity matrix and identify the accepted Safe to Host auditor.

## Guardrails

Tender fees and EMD stay in TVNL’s designated e-procurement/banking systems. Do not route regulated payments through a private gateway. Do not claim GIGW or STQC certification; facilitate assessment where contracted.

## Acceptance criteria

- TVNL signs off the procurement route and total-value treatment.
- Seller eligibility pack is complete.
- Hosting/data-location decision is written.
- Audit owner, scope, fees and pre-cutover clearance are recorded.
- Any commercial option is separated from the firm Phase 1 award.
