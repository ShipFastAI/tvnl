# TVNL modernization — plan and discovery

## Objective

Turn the TVNL website modernization proposal into a staged, evidence-led engagement. Start with the public static information site and preserve CMS, multi-site and payment work as separately approved options.

## Work

- Confirm TVNL content owner / Web Information Manager, IT lead, procurement lead and acceptance officer.
- Confirm hosting, DNS control, approved Indian hosting path and whether the CMS backend must also reside in India.
- Confirm TVNL service procurement route, delegation of powers, GeM category fit and any JAP-IT channel.
- Inventory live notices, reports, board/org content, approved Hindi copy, logos and image rights.
- Confirm static-export behavior, form/data requirements and security/audit prerequisites.
- Use the prototype and proposal package as review artifacts; do not present planning prices as an award or certification.

## Dependencies

Official TVNL content access, TVNL discovery meeting, seller eligibility/GST documentation, hosting decision and procurement guidance.

## Acceptance criteria

- Written answers recorded for discovery questions D1–D9 in `docs/tvnl.md`.
- Approved Phase 1 scope and migration manifest.
- Procurement route and total-value treatment confirmed by TVNL.
- Hosting/data-flow decision recorded before any firm production commitment.
- Package B/C remain written options until the relevant features are built, demonstrated and exercised.

## Evidence boundary

The repository contains the proposal prototype and draft documents. TVNL priorities, procurement route, production readiness and deployment acceptance remain unproven until TVNL confirms them.
