# TVNL modernization — expansion and reference playbook

## Objective

Use the TVNL engagement to create a reusable state-PSU digital-information playbook after the first deployment is accepted.

## Potential expansion

- Optional TVNL pages for the solar project, 2 × 660 MW expansion and Rajbar E&D coal-block progress, using approved published information.
- Multi-site management for related project or institutional sites.
- Structured RTI/public disclosure content and document search.
- JAP-IT empanelment through the formal process.
- Reference-led outreach to JBVNL, JUSNL, JREDA and comparable state power PSUs.
- Generic product improvements for organizations that publish reports, notices and time-sensitive documents.

## Boundaries

Project pages document approved progress; they do not claim to prevent coal-block deallocation, recover receivables, improve JSERC filings or replace project-management systems. No customer endorsement is used without written permission.

## Acceptance criteria

- TVNL Phase 1 acceptance and permission to reference are complete.
- Expansion scope has a separate estimate, owner and written approval.
- Multi-site and role workflows pass a real acceptance review before activation.
- The playbook contains generic patterns and no TVNL-only engine branches.
