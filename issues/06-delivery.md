# TVNL modernization — delivery and launch

## Objective

Deliver a defensible Phase 1 public-information site after procurement, approved content, Indian hosting and security gates are complete.

## Milestones

- M0: seller eligibility, scope, hosting, data and contacts approved.
- M1: sitemap, bilingual content matrix, migration manifest and policy responsibilities approved.
- M2: staging navigation, language, responsive, accessibility, search and document-download review.
- M3: host/region evidence, operational controls and accepted Safe to Host clearance.
- M4: authorized DNS cutover, live link/download checks, release export and runbook handoff.
- M5: annual support, update allowance, escalation and service records.

## Acceptance criteria

- Approved TVNL content is migrated within the agreed inventory.
- Public site is delivered to the agreed Indian host.
- Required disclosures, policies, reports and external procurement links are available.
- Hindi copy is approved by TVNL.
- Auditor/TVNL accepts the production release before cutover.
- DNS owner authorizes cutover and rollback materials are handed over.
- Training and support contacts are documented.

## Exclusions

SAP APIs, live operational data, employee/HR portal replacement, custom grievance intake, tender bid submission, EMD/tender-fee collection and unapproved payment processing.
