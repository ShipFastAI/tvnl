02 / Thermal Power Generation Process

From Coal to Grid:
The 8-Stage Thermal Process

An unconstrained engineering flow of Tenughat Thermal Power Station: as coal, air, and high-pressure steam transform into 210 MW of electrical power.

STAGE 01 — FUEL & COAL FEED

Coal Conveying &
Crusher House

Raw coal from captive coal blocks and railways is unloaded via wagon tipplers, crushed in the Coal Handling Plant (CHP), and transported via high-capacity conveyor belts directly to the boiler bunkers.

STAGE 02 — BOILER & COMBUSTION

1400°C Pulverized
Combustion

Heavy pulverizer bowl mills grind the coal into fine dust. Primary Air (PA) fans blow the pulverized coal into the 1400°C boiler furnace, releasing immense thermal energy.

STAGE 03 — STEAM GENERATION

535°C High-Pressure
Superheated Steam

Demineralized water in furnace wall tubes absorbs intense heat to produce saturated steam. The steam passes through superheater tube banks, raising its temperature to 535°C at 170 kg/cm² pressure.

STAGE 04 — STEAM TURBINES

3000 RPM Thermal
Kinetic Expansion

High-energy steam expands through High Pressure (HP), Intermediate Pressure (IP), and Low Pressure (LP) turbine stages, driving the central steel shaft at a precise 3000 RPM synchronous speed.

STAGE 05 — POWER GENERATION

210 MW Electromagnetic
Alternator

The rotating shaft turns the hydrogen-cooled 210 MW alternator, generating 15.75 kV 3-phase AC power. Main step-up transformers instantly elevate the voltage to 220 kV for efficient transmission.

STAGE 06 — CONDENSER & COOLING

Surface Condensation &
Reservoir Loop

Exhaust steam is condensed back into pure water in surface condensers using cooling water drawn from the adjacent Tenughat Reservoir. Hyperbolic cooling towers dissipate heat into the atmosphere.

STAGE 07 — ASH & EMISSION CONTROL

99.9% Electrostatic
Fly Ash Filtration

Flue gas passes through high-voltage Electrostatic Precipitators (ESP) that trap 99.9% of particulate fly ash before clean gas is discharged safely into the upper atmosphere through the 220m chimney stack.

STAGE 08 — STATE GRID TRANSMISSION

220kV High-Voltage
Distribution

220 kV power enters the switchyard and flows out into Jharkhand's state electrical grid, supplying reliable electricity to industrial hubs, cities, and millions of homes across the state.
